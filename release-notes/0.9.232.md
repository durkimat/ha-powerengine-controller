### Behaviour changes

- **Control no longer stops after taking over a Solis inverter.** When PowerEngine takes over it closes the timed windows and writes plain Self-Use. With every window closed the Solis settles on "Self-Use - No Timed Charge/Discharge" and the SolaX Modbus integration reports that on its next poll. PowerEngine only accepted the exact word it wrote, saw a mismatch twice, and stopped control until the engine was restarted: no charging or selling, while the dashboard still showed the plan. Plain Self-Use is now confirmed by either name (the Solis definition lists them as `self_use_equivalents`). Every other mode and value still has to read back exactly.
- Nothing else about what the engine decides changes.
