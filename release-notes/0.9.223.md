### Behaviour changes

- **The dashboard's charts are PowerEngine's own: the ApexCharts card is no longer needed.** The plan chart (next 36 hours,
  18 on a phone), the history chart, the house-load-and-solar chart (the Engine v1 page) and the Health page's system-losses
  chart are drawn by PowerEngine's card (`custom:powerengine-chart-card`, `chart: plan | history | load_solar | losses`).
  Same series, colours, axes, dashes and "Now" marker; point at or touch a chart to read the values at that time (the plan
  chart still says what the engine planned to do and why); tick or untick a line in the key to hide it (remembered in this
  browser). A phone gets one axis and a shorter span by itself, so each chart is one card, not a desktop copy and a phone
  copy. You can uninstall ApexCharts from HACS if nothing of yours uses it: the dashboard no longer does.
- The setup checklist no longer lists the chart card; it still lists the Sunsynk Power Flow card (the energy-flow picture on
  Monitoring), which is the one third-party card left and the next to be replaced.

### Behind the scenes

- `engine/dashboard/dashboard.yaml` loses the eight desktop/phone ApexCharts blocks (about 550 lines of series, data
  generators and tooltip scripts) for four `powerengine-chart-card` entries. The data generators became plain functions in
  the card, tested against the sensors' attributes (`card/tests/charts.test.cjs`: windows, ticks, axis ranges, smooth and
  step paths, the model for each chart, the readout), and the charts were checked in a browser against the live sensors at
  desktop and phone widths.
- The dashboard test now checks that no chart card of another install is used and that every chart is one the card knows.
