### Behaviour changes

- **An Axle outage around 03:00 no longer stops the battery's control.** The grid-event sensors (Axle) go unavailable for a few minutes most nights, and the car charger's sometimes does too. The app used to declare its inputs "not ready" and switch remote control Off, handing the battery to Self-Use in the middle of the overnight charge (31 Oct, 03:06). It now holds each grid-event sensor's last reading for 15 minutes when it reports "unavailable" ("unknown", which means no event is scheduled, is not held), and after that carries on assuming no event is running instead of stopping control. The Health tab still shows the input as unavailable.
- **Cost records say what time they lacked.** Each half-hour's record now notes the seconds that were not measured and why: which of grid, battery or house power was missing, a gap of over five minutes (the app stalled or restarted), or the app starting part-way through. The diagnostics file has a new `coverage` section with each of the last 14 days' coverage, the minutes lost by reason and the worst half-hours, so an incomplete day explains itself.
- **More days can be replayed in the engine comparison.** A day measured for at least 90% of its seconds (it was 95%) is accepted, with each thin half-hour scaled up to the full half-hour from what was measured. A half-hour measured for under ten minutes still refuses the day.
