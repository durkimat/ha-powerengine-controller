### Behaviour changes

- **Why a day fell short.** In "Engines compared", select a compared day to open a breakdown: where engine v2's cost differed from the best possible (the grid event, overnight, daytime, evening, and the battery level it left), a few plain sentences about it (for example how much v2 exported in the event against the best possible and what the real system sent), and the numbers behind them. Days compared before tonight have no breakdown; they get one if they are compared again.
- **Grid events by month.** In "Special events", select a month to see the days that had an event, with each day's kWh, payment, energy used and net. The last three months are listed.

### Behind the scenes

- `compare/why.py`; the replay costs every half-hour of v2's run and of the best possible (`halves`), and result files carry `why`. `Costbook.months` lists `ev_days` for the last three months; `sensor.pe_event_months` carries `names`. New card `powerengine-events-card`; needs card 0.9.186.
