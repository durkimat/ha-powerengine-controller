### Behaviour changes

None. Engine v1 and engine v2 decisions are unchanged.

### New

- **Engine v2 now publishes the sun and the house load its plan was made with.** The expected-timeline sensor
  (`sensor.pe_v2_timeline`) gains a `sun` attribute: average kW for each half hour of the look-ahead, with the low,
  middle and high sun forecast and the expected house load (the same figures the plan was worked out on, after any
  learned correction). The Engine v2 page's chart draws it (needs the matching card).
