### Behaviour changes

None to how PowerEngine controls the battery or the inverter. This only changes what the Engine v1 page's History chart shows.

### Other changes

- **The History chart can look ahead.** The next arrow now carries on past today, up to two days into the plan (as far as the plan reaches). Today shows what ran so far, solid, and the rest of the day from the current plan, dashed. A day still to come is the plan alone, labelled "Forecast". A red **Now** line marks the present moment on today.
- **Grid charging and exports show when they really happened.** The green charge, red battery-export and yellow solar-export areas were half-hour blocks. They are now drawn from Home Assistant's recorded grid and battery power in 5-minute steps, so they start and stop when the power did, and cover the same energy. A day Home Assistant no longer holds history for (or a failed read) falls back to the half-hour blocks. The history is read once for a past day and every few minutes for today.
- **Pick a time and see the values.** A slider under the battery chart steps through the half-hours and lists the plan and the actual figures for the one you choose (battery, price, grid charge, exports, house load, solar), with the plan's action and why. It opens on the present half-hour on today. It needs the card released with this version and hides itself on an older card.
- The History data refreshes every 5 minutes instead of 15.
- To check after updating: step the History day forward past today and back; the Now line sits at the present time only on today; a past day's green, red and yellow areas begin and end at sensible times; the slider's numbers match the chart. If the green/red/yellow areas look wrong on your install, say so: the grid and battery power sensors are what they are drawn from.
