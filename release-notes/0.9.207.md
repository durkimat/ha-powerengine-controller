### Behaviour changes

- The Costs sensors (`sensor.pe_cost_today`, `pe_cost_saved_today`, `pe_cost_days`, `pe_cost_waterfall`, and the
  simulator and engine comparison ones) are now published when entities are published directly rather than through
  MQTT. A leftover check from before direct publishing made the app skip them whenever it had no MQTT connection, so
  they stayed `unknown`. Nothing changes with MQTT publishing, where they were always published.

### Behind the scenes

- Found in the shadow run of the Home Assistant integration (which has no MQTT connection). A new test in
  `tests/test_startup.py` publishes the cost sensors with no MQTT handle.
