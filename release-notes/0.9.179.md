### Behaviour changes

- **Heating set-back for grid events (off until you switch it on).** With the gas boiler off, the central heating still draws a few hundred watts, probably the circulation pump, and every unit not used during a grid event is exported at the event rate. On the Config page, under Grid events, "Turn the heating down before grid events" turns a switch of yours on shortly before an event and off when it ends. Choose the switch under "Heating set-back switch" (a Home Assistant switch or input_boolean, for example `input_boolean.heating_off`, which holds every room at frost protection) and the lead time under "Heating pump overrun" (15 minutes by default): the switch goes on that long before the event starts, so the pump has stopped by the time it begins.
- **It is careful with your switch.** A switch that is already on is left alone and not switched off afterwards; if you switch it off yourself during the set-back, it stays off for that event; it is switched off at the end only if it still reads on, and a restart in the middle of an event still switches it off. Active mode only.
- The card (0.9.179, included) shows the new switch, role and setting; an older card is warned about, because its save would drop them.

### Behind the scenes
