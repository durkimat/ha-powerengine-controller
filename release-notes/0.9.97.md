### Behaviour changes

- A reading that goes missing for up to three minutes (for example the import rate, which dropped out for one minute on 4 Oct) no longer hands the inverter to Self-use and straight back. PowerEngine keeps its last decision through the gap. A longer gap behaves as before.

### Other changes

- Plan history, "As run": the day no longer lists every window a replan replaced (52 windows, 17 KB, over Home Assistant's 16 KB limit, so its history was not recorded). It now shows the windows that actually ran, merged, and is held under the limit.
